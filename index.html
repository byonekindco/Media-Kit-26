01 — Who We Are

A media and experiences
company. Not a platform.

"We built the newsletter before we had a following, the dinners before we had a venue, and the slams before we had a stage. Everything here was built because the community needed it — not because a pitch deck said to."

OneKind Co is a media and experiences company for Black women who are building something real — not aspirationally, not in the future, but right now. The newsletter, events, and community programming we run exist because the founders in this ecosystem needed them to.

We are not a conference company. We are not an influencer platform. We are not a sponsorship vehicle dressed up as community. We're a media brand with a growing, highly engaged audience of Black women entrepreneurs — and we're selective about the brands that show up in our rooms.

Everything at OneKind Co is built to last. The properties in this kit are the first chapter of a multi-year ecosystem. Brands that partner with us now are in the room before the world finds it.

That's the value, and it's real.

06 — How We Work

Brands are chosen.
Not sold slots.

Community first
Every brand in our rooms has been vetted against a simple question: would the women in this room be glad this brand is here? If the answer is anything other than yes, we don't move forward — regardless of budget.
Founder-to-founder tone
We work with brands whose decision-makers are willing to have a real conversation about fit. We don't work with brands that want to manage the activation, bring their own marketing team to the event, or treat this like a media buy. This is a partnership, not a transaction.
Editorial independence
Sponsoring an Un/Read issue does not give you editorial input. The newsletter's credibility is the value you're buying — we don't compromise it for anyone. Sponsors are disclosed; influence over content is not on the table.
Honest about fit
If your brand isn't the right fit for a specific property, we'll tell you. If we think there's a better way to structure the partnership, we'll say so. We'd rather lose the deal than put the wrong brand in the room.

"We're not putting your logo on a banner. We're putting your product in the hands of 15 women who will talk about it for weeks."

Not the right fit if you're looking for
  • Guaranteed impression numbers or reach minimums
  • Editorial control over how your brand is mentioned
  • Brands that want to bring their own team to "manage the activation"
  • Demographic marketing dressed up as community partnership
  • A one-time transaction with no interest in the community itself
